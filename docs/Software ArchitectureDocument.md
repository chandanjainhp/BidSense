# 1. Introduction

## 1.1 Project Name

**BidSense – AI-Powered Procurement & RFP Management Platform**

---

## 1.2 Purpose

BidSense is a modern Software-as-a-Service (SaaS) platform that simplifies the complete Request for Proposal (RFP) lifecycle using Artificial Intelligence.

Organizations can create RFPs, invite vendors, receive proposals, compare submissions, evaluate compliance, communicate with vendors, and make procurement decisions from a single platform.

The platform integrates AI to automate repetitive procurement tasks, improve decision quality, and reduce manual effort.

---

## 1.3 Problem Statement

Most procurement processes are fragmented across spreadsheets, emails, PDF documents, and multiple software systems.

Common challenges include:

* Manual RFP creation
* Vendor management across multiple systems
* Proposal comparison requiring significant human effort
* Lack of intelligent recommendations
* No centralized procurement workflow
* Limited analytics
* Poor collaboration between stakeholders
* Difficulty tracking procurement history

BidSense addresses these challenges by providing a centralized, AI-assisted procurement platform.

---

## 1.4 Vision

Build a production-grade procurement platform that combines traditional procurement workflows with Artificial Intelligence to improve efficiency, transparency, and decision-making.

The long-term vision is to evolve BidSense into an enterprise procurement ecosystem supporting organizations of all sizes.

---

## 1.5 Objectives

The primary objectives are:

* Simplify RFP creation.
* Centralize vendor management.
* Automate proposal evaluation.
* Enable AI-assisted procurement decisions.
* Reduce procurement turnaround time.
* Improve compliance verification.
* Provide detailed procurement analytics.
* Support secure multi-organization collaboration.
* Deliver an extensible architecture for future AI capabilities.

---

## 1.6 Target Users

### Procurement Managers

Responsible for creating RFPs, inviting vendors, and evaluating proposals.

### Procurement Teams

Collaborate throughout the procurement lifecycle.

### Vendors

Receive invitations, submit proposals, and communicate with procurement teams.

### Organization Administrators

Manage users, permissions, subscriptions, and organizational settings.

### Executives

Monitor procurement performance through dashboards and analytics.
